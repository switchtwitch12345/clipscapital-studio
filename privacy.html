Privacy Policy

Applies to: Clips Capital Studio, a self-hosted video clipping and uploading tool operated by an individual for their own YouTube channel.
Last updated: 26 November 2026

Who uses this app

This app has exactly one user: its operator. It is not offered to the public, there is no sign-up, and it runs only on the operator's own computer. It is not a hosted service.

What Google / YouTube data the app accesses

When the operator connects their own YouTube channel with Google sign-in, the app requests two permissions:

  • youtube.upload: to upload short videos the operator has made to the operator's own channel.
  • youtube.readonly: to read the connected channel's name and ID, the processing status of videos it uploaded, and their view counts.

The app does not read, collect or process any data about other YouTube users or viewers, and does not access comments, subscribers, messages or analytics beyond public view counts of the operator's own uploads.

How the data is stored

The OAuth access and refresh tokens, the channel name and ID, and the IDs, statuses and view counts of uploaded videos are stored only on the operator's own computer, in the app's local data folder. Nothing is sent to any server other than Google's APIs.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared, or transferred to any third party. No advertising, analytics or tracking services are used.

Retention and deletion

Data is kept only while the channel stays connected. Disconnecting the channel in the app deletes the stored tokens and channel details. Access can also be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions, which immediately stops all access.

Google policies

This app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy. The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
