Clips Capital Studio

Clips Capital Studio is a self-hosted tool that one creator uses to turn long-form footage they are authorised to use into short vertical clips, and to upload those clips to their own YouTube channel.

It runs only on the operator's own computer. It is not a public service and has no other users.

What it does

  • Takes a source video file the operator is authorised to clip (for example footage supplied by the rights holder for a clipping campaign).
  • Finds highlight moments, crops them to 9:16 and adds captions.
  • After the operator reviews a clip, uploads it to the operator's own YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API, and reads back its processing status and view count.
